# Product

## Register

product

## Users

A single owner using the secretary from a phone or computer, often while rushed, mobile, or multitasking. They need to speak naturally, hear an immediate response, and complete calendar, reminder, contact, route, booking, and memory tasks without navigating a conventional dashboard.

## Product Purpose

Secretary AI is a low-latency voice interface for personal administration. It succeeds when starting a conversation feels as immediate as answering a phone call, interruptions work naturally, useful actions are completed safely through the existing backend, and Telegram is optional rather than part of the conversational critical path.

## Brand Personality

Calm, precise, discreet. The voice and interface should feel capable without being theatrical, reassuring without becoming chatty, and polished without drawing attention away from the task.

## Anti-references

Not a science-fiction assistant, glowing AI orb showcase, glassmorphism dashboard, generic chatbot, or dense developer console. Avoid decorative telemetry, fake waveform spectacle, excessive animation, and controls that make a conversation feel like operating software.

## Design Principles

- Conversation is the interface: make starting, interrupting, muting, and ending unmistakable.
- Latency is a product feature: reduce hops, reveal connection state honestly, and never block speech on ornamental motion.
- Quiet confidence: use familiar call behavior, concise language, and restrained feedback.
- Actions remain trustworthy: distinguish listening, thinking, speaking, confirmation, success, and failure without ambiguity.
- Progressive depth: keep the primary call surface minimal while transcripts and diagnostics remain available when needed.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Meet WCAG 2.2 AA for contrast, semantics, focus visibility, keyboard navigation, and screen-reader announcements. Support reduced motion, large touch targets, zoom and narrow viewports, captions/transcripts, non-color state cues, and clear recovery from microphone, network, authentication, and permission failures.
